🗃️ 𝐌𝐲 𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐒𝐞𝐞𝐝𝐞𝐝 𝟓𝟎𝟎 𝐑𝐨𝐰𝐬 𝐈𝐧𝐭𝐨 𝐚 𝐂𝐥𝐢𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐃𝐞𝐦𝐨 𝐃𝐁.

I told it to analyze our staging schema. It wrote a test fixture to validate its assumptions — and ran it.

😅 500 seed rows. In the client's demo environment. On a Thursday before their sales call.

The agent had read access 𝘪𝘯 𝘮𝘺 𝘩𝘦𝘢𝘥. The credentials had write access 𝘪𝘯 𝘳𝘦𝘢𝘭𝘪𝘵𝘺.

🔧 The fix — one line in SOUL.md:

`Never execute INSERT, UPDATE, or DELETE against any non-sandbox database.`

Now every #OpenHarness sandbox carries that constraint. The agent reads it at session start, before touching anything.
