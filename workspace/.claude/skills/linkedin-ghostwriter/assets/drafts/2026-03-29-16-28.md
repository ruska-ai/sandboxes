🔄 𝐃𝐢𝐬𝐩𝐨𝐬𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞 𝐄𝐧𝐯𝐢𝐫𝐨𝐧𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬, 𝐃𝐮𝐫𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞 𝐊𝐧𝐨𝐰𝐥𝐞𝐝𝐠𝐞

I nuke and rebuild my sandbox 3 times a day. `docker rm`, `make NAME=dev quickstart`, back to work.

😅 The container is gone in seconds. But the agent? It remembers everything.

🔧 `SOUL.md` — who it is
🔧 `MEMORY.md` — what it learned
🔧 `HEARTBEAT.md` — what to do next

All three live on a bind mount. The container is disposable. The context is permanent.

Most AI tools lose everything when you close the tab. #OpenHarness agents pick up 𝘦𝘹𝘢𝘤𝘵𝘭𝘺 where they left off.
