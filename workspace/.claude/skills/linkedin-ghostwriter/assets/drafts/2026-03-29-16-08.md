Last week I ran 5 #OpenHarness sandboxes on one VPS — research, frontend, backend, docs, and CI.

```bash
make NAME=research quickstart
make NAME=frontend quickstart
make NAME=ci quickstart
```

🔧 Each gets its own workspace, its own `MEMORY.md`, its own heartbeat schedule. Five independent agents, zero crosstalk.

😅 4GB of RAM. I budgeted for crashes. Instead, idle sandboxes barely sip memory between heartbeat cycles.

📌 The `make NAME=` pattern is the whole trick — named containers with bind-mounted workspaces that survive restarts.
